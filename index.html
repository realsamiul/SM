§ 01 — The Thesis

Every country has
the same AI problems.
Only ours has
$1.60 solutions.

The standard playbook for "AI for development" fails because the infrastructure assumptions don't transfer. Bangladeshi garment factories don't have $12,000 optical inspection rigs. 78% of the rural population owns a button phone, not a smartphone.

My approach is different: study the best‑in‑class solution, identify its infrastructure assumptions, and re‑architect from first principles for the actual constraints of South Asia. That produces systems 10–80× cheaper to deploy while maintaining competitive accuracy.

AI is the most powerful tool in human history that remains almost entirely unavailable to the people who need it most. That is a design problem. I build the designs.

"The insight from the garment factory: India sells ready-made optical inspection for crores. I rebuilt it on a recycled Android phone at $150 CAPEX. The factory doesn't care who made the component — it cares about the margin."
